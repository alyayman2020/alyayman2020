I design AI systems that are **grounded, measurable, and safe to ship**: retrieval pipelines that abstain when evidence is weak, agents with explicit guardrails, and every model decision traceable through evaluation panels, audit logs, and version stamps.

My path is deliberately cross-disciplinary:

- 🤖 **AI engineering** → ITI Data Science Track graduate, Academic Diploma in Data Science (GPA 3.78), MSc in Data Science at FGSSR starting Oct 2026
